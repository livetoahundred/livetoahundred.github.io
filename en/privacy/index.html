Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026 · Türkçe (the Turkish text governs)

In short. livetoahundred publishes research-based health posts, and short explainer videos made from them. Our publishing app posts only to our own accounts. It also reads the statistics of our own accounts and posts: totals such as views, likes and the number of comments, and the breakdown of our followers as a group by country, city, age and gender, as the platform reports it. It does not read comments or messages, and it collects nothing that identifies the people who see, follow or interact with our posts. If you subscribe to the newsletter, we take only your e-mail address. This website uses no cookies and no analytics. Questions or requests: info.live2a100@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

livetoahundred is run by one private individual living in Türkiye; it is not a company. It publishes as @livetoahundred on Instagram and on our Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube accounts. That person is the data controller (veri sorumlusu) under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK).

Contact: info.live2a100@gmail.com. Anyone who makes a request under section 10, and any competent authority, will be given the controller's full name and postal address on request, without delay.

2. What this policy covers

  • this website;
  • our publishing app, which runs on our own server: the Meta app livetoa100 (its Instagram part is called livetoa100-IG) for our Instagram professional account @livetoahundred and our Facebook Page, and the LinkedIn app livetoa100. It also uses the Zernio posting service for our TikTok and YouTube accounts;
  • the explainer videos we make from our posts and publish as Instagram and Facebook Reels and as TikTok videos (section 4);
  • our newsletter subscription (section 7);
  • e-mails you send us.

It does not cover Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube or Telegram themselves. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

What the app never does. Meta's standard Instagram setup includes permissions to manage comments and messages (instagram_business_manage_comments, instagram_business_manage_messages). To publish to a Facebook Page, Meta requires four more permissions: pages_show_list and business_management, which let the app find our own Page, and pages_manage_posts and pages_read_engagement, which let it post, check that the post went out and read the reaction, comment and share counts of our own posts. read_insights lets it read our own Page's statistics. The app uses them only for our own Page. It does not read, store or answer comments or direct messages, and it does not collect data about individual followers, people who like or comment, or viewers: the statistics it reads are totals and percentages computed by the platform, with no names or user IDs. If we ever want to use the comment or message permissions, we will update this policy before we start.

We do not sell, rent or give away platform data. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not combine it with other data.

4. Research sources and people named in them

Our posts summarise published studies and news articles. To write a post, our tool downloads the study or article. That includes the author list and any contact details printed in it. The tool sends the text to an AI language model provider (currently OpenAI) to draft the post. A post may name authors, journals or people in the news, and it credits its source. We use this public information only to report on the work. KVKK Art. 28(1)(c) exempts processing for scientific purposes or within freedom of expression, provided it does not violate privacy or personal rights, and we keep within that limit.

Explainer videos. For some posts we also make a short video that explains the post. Anthropic (Claude) writes the script from the post and its source text. OpenAI draws the illustrations. ElevenLabs reads the narration with a synthetic voice. We put the video together on our own server: the illustrations stay still, and any charts are drawn there from the post's numbers. The videos show drawn characters, not real people, and they say in their caption that they were made with artificial intelligence. No personal data of readers or followers goes into them.

The full texts saved with each post are deleted from our server after 30 days. The title and abstract of every study the tool has considered, and up to 12,000 characters of the text of every news article it has considered, stay in our database as a record of what it has already seen. If you are named in a post and something is wrong, write to us and we will correct it.

5. Our private review chat on Telegram

Before a post is published, it goes to a private Telegram group that only people we invite can join. There it is approved, rejected or corrected. Instructions typed in that group are stored in our database for as long as we run the account, and they may be sent to the AI model provider to carry out the request. We do not store the names or Telegram user IDs of group members. Telegram keeps the chat history under its own policy.

6. Where data is kept and for how long

  • Access tokens and account IDs. They are stored on our own server in files that only our own server account can read. We keep them until they are replaced. We delete them when we remove the app's access to an account or stop using a platform, or when you ask (see Data deletion).
  • Publishing records (which post went to which platform, its post ID and its status). These stay in our database for as long as we run the account.
  • Working files for each post (images, video, captions and source text). These are deleted from the server after 30 days. For explainer videos, the working files (script drafts, narration, illustrations and clips) are deleted 30 days after the video is made; the finished video and its script are kept with the post. We also keep one archive of post files from 3 to 7 September 2026 (images, video and captions, without source texts) on the same server, with no set deletion date. A published post stays on the platform until we delete it there.
  • Statistics (see section 3). They are recorded once a day for our accounts and several times a day for recent posts, and stay in our database for as long as we run the account.
  • Database backups. These are kept on the same server for 14 days.
  • Newsletter subscribers. E-mail addresses and subscription records (confirmation date and IP address) are kept at Brevo until you unsubscribe. After that, the address stays only on a do-not-send list, so that you are not e-mailed again. Write to us if you want it removed completely.
  • Media for TikTok and YouTube. This is uploaded to Zernio and kept under Zernio's own terms. For Instagram, post images may be placed at a public web address so that Instagram can fetch them. Videos for Instagram and TikTok are uploaded to Zernio, which keeps them at a temporary public web address for 7 days so that the platforms can fetch them.

7. This website

This website sets no cookies. It has no analytics or tracking, and it loads no third-party scripts or fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). GitHub logs every visitor's IP address for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub. We do not receive those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Newsletter subscription. The only form on this website is the newsletter signup. When you submit it, your e-mail address goes straight to our e-mail service provider, Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which also records your IP address and the time. You then get a confirmation e-mail; you are not subscribed unless you click its link. We use your address only to send the newsletter; we do not share it or use it for advertising. We do not track opens or clicks in our newsletters. Every issue has a one-click unsubscribe link.

8. E-mails you send us

If you write to us, we receive your address and your message through Gmail (Google). We use them only to answer you, and we delete them within 12 months of the conversation ending. The exception is a request under section 10: we keep it, with our answer, for as long as a complaint about it could still be made. Please do not send us your health information. If you do, we do not use it in our reply, and we delete it after replying.

10. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you may ask us:

  • whether we process your personal data, and for information about it;
  • for what purpose we process it, and whether it is used for that purpose;
  • which third parties in Türkiye or abroad receive it;
  • to correct it if it is incomplete or wrong, and to delete or destroy it;
  • to tell the recipients about those corrections and deletions;
  • to object to a result against you that comes solely from automated analysis;
  • for compensation if unlawful processing causes you damage.

Readers in the EU also have the GDPR rights to restriction, portability and objection.

How to apply. E-mail info.live2a100@gmail.com in Turkish or English. Give your name and say what you are asking for. We accept requests by e-mail. We may ask for information to confirm that the request is yours. We answer free of charge within 30 days.

Complaints. You may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu) at sikayet.kvkk.gov.tr if we reject your request, answer it inadequately or do not answer in time. The deadline is 30 days after our answer, and no later than 60 days after your request. Readers in the EU may also complain to their local data protection authority.
