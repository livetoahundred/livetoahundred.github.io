Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated 24 October 2026 · Türkçe (the Turkish text governs)

Our publishing app, livetoa100 (Instagram: livetoa100-IG), acts only on accounts owned by livetoahundred. It reads only aggregate statistics for our own accounts and posts, and collects nothing that identifies the people who see, follow or comment on our posts. Anyone can still ask us to delete data, and this page explains how.

1. Remove the app's access yourself

If you ever connected an account to the app, you can remove its access at any time. The app's access token for that account stops working at once. Removing access does not by itself delete what the app has stored; to have that deleted, follow section 2 below.

  • Instagram: Settings and activity → Website permissions → Apps and websites → Active → livetoa100-IG → Remove.
  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (for Pages: Business integrations) → livetoa100 → Remove.
  • LinkedIn: Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Permitted services → Change → livetoa100 → Remove.

The menu names can differ slightly between app versions.

2. Ask us to delete data

  1. E-mail info.live2a100@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Tell us your name and the account the request is about: your Instagram username, Facebook name or Page, or LinkedIn profile. If it is about a post or about an e-mail you sent, tell us which one. Say what you want deleted, or write “everything”.
  3. If we need to confirm that the request is yours, we will ask.

What we delete: access tokens, account and Page IDs, names, publishing records and e-mails that relate to you. We delete them from our server promptly, and within 30 days at the latest. A LinkedIn access token, and anything else the app received from LinkedIn, is deleted as soon as we receive the request. Copies in our daily database backups disappear within the next 15 days as the backups roll over. When we are done, we confirm by e-mail. If the law requires us to keep something, we tell you what it is and why.

Posts on social media. If one of our posts names you and you want it corrected or removed, tell us which post. We will fix or delete it on the platform. Data held by Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Telegram or Zernio is governed by their own policies.

More about what we process is in our Privacy Policy.
