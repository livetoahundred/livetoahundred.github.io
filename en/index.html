HOW WE WORK

Four rules we keep.

  1. 01

    If the evidence says no, so do we.

    “It didn’t” is an answer too. We never inflate a finding or present an association as cause and effect.

  2. 02

    An animal study isn’t a human finding.

    We never write up a mouse or cell result as if it were proven in people, and we say who was studied.

  3. 03

    Every number has a source.

    Every number on a card comes from the source. If you spot a mistake, write to us and we’ll fix it.

  4. 04

    Written in Turkish, not translated.

    We rewrite the research in Turkish rather than translating it, and explain each term where it first appears: VO2max (peak oxygen uptake).
